Our Story

Rooted in Craft,
Lit by Passion

Viva Coffee was born from a simple conviction: that the oldest cooking technique — fire — is still the most profound. Head Chef Marcus Holloway spent a decade in kitchens across Lyon, Copenhagen, and Tokyo before returning to New York to open this intimate 42-seat restaurant in 2012.

We build every menu around what the season gives us, not what the market demands. Our suppliers are our partners; many have been with us since opening night.

  • Open-Flame Kitchen

    Every protein is kissed by wood fire, imparting irreplaceable depth and char.

  • Farm-to-Fork Philosophy

    Partnerships with 14 local farms ensure freshness within 48 hours of harvest.

  • Award-Winning Experience

    Three Michelin stars, James Beard finalist, and #4 on Eater's New York Top 50.
